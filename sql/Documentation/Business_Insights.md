# Project 04: Validated Business Insights

- The operation recorded **$2.381B revenue and $242.78M profit** across 10,500 consultant-week observations.
- Average capacity utilization is **77.89%**, indicating aggregate headroom; the management question is therefore **where** capacity is constrained rather than whether the whole organization needs more people.
- **Nordics** has the highest average utilization at about **78.90%**, followed by Middle East and Europe. No vertical averages above the 80% Healthy threshold in the validated dataset.
- Profitability is below the configured target in every vertical, making productivity alone insufficient: staffing and workflow decisions should also consider margin quality.
- The request-event layer contains **300,000 events** and a **17.78% SLA breach rate**, with **$67.15M revenue impact**.
- Standard vendors show the highest vendor-week breach rate at roughly **61.20%**, followed closely by Strategic and Preferred vendors. Vendor type alone is therefore not enough; individual vendor performance should drive the watchlist.
- Priority-level SLA breach rates are surprisingly similar across Urgent, High, Normal and Low work, suggesting that the operating issue is systemic rather than limited to urgent requests.
- The project should be used to identify **specific verticals/consultants/vendors where capacity, workload, SLA risk and profitability intersect**, rather than recommending blanket headcount increases.
